The First Timer Platform successfully addresses **approximately 75%** of the Telepastoring Department's manual requirements with strong foundations in:

- **Data management and organization**
- **Follow-up tracking and accountability**
- **User role management and security**

The platform provides an **excellent foundation** that can be enhanced through phased development to fully support the department's vision and mission. With focused development on the identified gaps, the system can achieve **100% alignment** with manual requirements while maintaining its technical excellence and scalability.

The implementation roadmap ensures that critical operational needs are addressed first, followed by enhancement features that will further empower the department's mission of ensuring "nobody is left out."
